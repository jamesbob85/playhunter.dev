The roguelike is the perfect phone genre hiding in plain sight: runs that fit a commute, depth that survives years, and no live-service strings — your progress is skill, not a battle pass. These four are the ones to own. Each is a one-time purchase, each is a different flavor of "one more run," and each will quietly become the most-opened app on your phone for a month.
