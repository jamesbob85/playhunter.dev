Stardew Valley is a farming and life sim in which you abandon a soul-crushing office job for a dilapidated farm in a small town. What starts as clearing rocks and planting parsnips unfolds into a game about everything: fishing, mining, monster caves, marriage, festivals, and the slow restoration of a community — all on a daily rhythm that makes "just one more day" the most dangerous sentence on Android.

It's made almost entirely by one person, and it shows in the best way: every system connects to every other with handcrafted intent. A decade after release it still tops "best mobile game" lists because nothing else matches its density of things worth doing.

The phone version is a genuinely great port — touch controls with multiple layout options, auto-attack toggles, and cloud-friendly save handling. **It costs $4.99 once, and every update since launch has been free.**
