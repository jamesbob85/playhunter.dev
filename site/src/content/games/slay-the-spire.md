Slay the Spire is the deckbuilding roguelike — the 2019 original that created the genre's grammar. You climb a three-act spire, fighting monsters in turn-based card combat, adding cards and relics as you go. Build synergies, dodge traps, and either ascend gloriously or collapse under a deck you overfed.

Its genius is restraint: fights are puzzles with visible enemy intents, so every loss decomposes into specific mistakes you can name. That's why it eats hundreds of hours — you're not grinding, you're getting measurably smarter.

The mobile version carries the complete PC experience — all four characters and the full Ascension ladder — for **$9.99, once**. In a genre now crowded with gacha imitations, the original remains the only one with nothing else to sell you.
