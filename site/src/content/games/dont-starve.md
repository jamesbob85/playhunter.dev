Don't Starve is a survival game drawn like a cursed storybook — all scratchy ink and wrong angles — in which a gentleman scientist is dumped into a wilderness that operates on hostile, hidden rules. Gather, craft, and prepare for a winter you won't survive the first time. Or the second.

Its signature move is treating your sanity as a survival meter alongside hunger: stay in the dark too long, eat the wrong things, and the hallucinations stop being hallucinations. Few games commit this completely to an aesthetic, and fewer make failure feel this fair in hindsight.

The Pocket Edition brings the full base game plus Reign of Giants content to touch for **$4.99, once**. It remains the best-looking way to die repeatedly on a phone.
