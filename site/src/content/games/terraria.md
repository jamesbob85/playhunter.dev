Terraria is a 2D sandbox where mining, building, crafting, and boss-fighting feed one endless loop. Dig into a generated world, smelt what you find, forge better gear, and wake progressively angrier gods — while building anything from a dirt hovel to a functioning city between fights.

Fourteen years of continuous free updates have made it grotesquely large: over 5,000 items, dozens of bosses and events, entire hidden biomes. The developers kept shipping content to every platform — including this one — without ever charging again.

At **$4.99 with no ads and no IAP**, it may be the single most generous purchase in mobile gaming. The touch controls take an evening to internalize; the game takes years to exhaust.
