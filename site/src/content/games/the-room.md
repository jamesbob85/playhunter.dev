The Room is a tactile puzzle game about opening an impossible box. Left alone in an attic with a safe and a dead man's notes, you pry at seams, rotate the whole apparatus, and discover mechanisms nested inside mechanisms — every one operated by direct touch: pinch, twist, slide, wind.

Fireproof Games built it specifically for glass. A decade of imitators later it remains the reference for what touch controls can feel like when a studio treats the screen as a material instead of a compromise. The eyepiece — which reveals inscriptions floating outside normal sight — still produces a genuine shiver.

At **$0.99** it's practically a formality. Buy it, turn the lights down, use headphones.
