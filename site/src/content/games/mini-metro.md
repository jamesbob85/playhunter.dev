Mini Metro is a strategy game disguised as a transit diagram. Stations bloom across a stylized city; you link them with colored lines, allocate your scarce trains and tunnels, and redraw everything as demand shifts. When a station overcrowds, the run ends — and you immediately start another, because the failure was so legible you can already see the better map.

It's the rare game that works identically as a two-minute palate cleanser and a two-hour trance. The design has the confidence of a Vignelli poster: nothing decorative, everything meaningful, right down to the Balearic click of a train docking.

At **$0.99 with nothing else to buy**, it's the cheapest complete argument for paid mobile games — proof that a dollar can buy design this considered.
