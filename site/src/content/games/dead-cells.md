Dead Cells is a roguelike action-platformer — a "roguevania" — where you fight through a cursed island as a blob of cells piloting a corpse. Runs are fast, brutal, and permadeath; what persists between them is a slow drip of permanent unlocks and, more importantly, your own growing mastery of its razor-sharp combat.

On a phone this should not work. It does, remarkably: Playdigious rebuilt the controls for touch with the kind of care most studios reserve for full sequels, and the 60fps combat survives intact. It has become the standard answer to "which action game should I actually buy on Android?"

**The $7.99 is the whole base game.** The in-app purchases you'll see listed are real DLC expansions — new biomes and bosses — not energy, gems, or anything that smells like a free-to-play economy.
