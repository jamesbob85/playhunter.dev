Balatro is a single-player deckbuilding roguelike that uses poker hands as raw material. Each round, you play hands from a standard 52-card deck to hit a score target — but the real game is the Jokers: over 150 rule-warping modifiers that multiply, mutate, and stack until your scoring math goes vertical. Beat the blind, spend your winnings in the shop, and push deeper toward boss blinds that bend the rules against you.

It was 2024's improbable phenomenon — a solo developer's first game that swept Game of the Year lists and sold millions of copies. The Android version isn't a port so much as a homecoming: runs are twenty minutes, the interface was rebuilt for thumbs, and the CRT-fuzz pixel art looks unreasonably good on an OLED phone.

**There is nothing to buy after you buy it.** No energy meters, no ads, no season pass. The $9.99 you pay on Google Play is the last money it will ever ask you for — which, in the paid-mobile landscape, is half the pitch.
