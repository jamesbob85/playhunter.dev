Monument Valley is the perspective-puzzle game that redefined what a phone game could be in 2014: guide the silent princess Ida through Escher-inspired monuments by rotating structures until optical illusions become real paths. If two platforms look connected from your angle, they are — the geometry obeys your eye, not physics.

It swept every design award available, showed up in House of Cards, and became the default answer to "show me a beautiful phone game." The panoramic remaster keeps it crisp on modern displays.

The base game is **$3.99 with two small expansion packs as optional IAP** — the 2014 model, quaint by today's standards. Ninety minutes of it will outlast most hundred-hour games in your memory.
