About

Game industry intelligence for the people who make them.

PlayHunter is an independent publication. We cover the business of indie games — where the market is going, who's getting the timing right, and what the data says about the decisions studios are making.

We don't cover console wars, review scores, or consumer drama. We cover the decisions that happen inside studios, the market context those decisions depend on, and what it costs to get the timing wrong.
