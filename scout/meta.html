How clusters work

A cluster is a thematic frame — not just a genre. Friendslop means cooperative-horror-with-mates as a marketing-first design choice. Sim-revival means the deliberate return to depth-rich management games after a decade of streamlining. Single-player narrative means the post-2020 willingness to spend $40–70M on a pure story product. Clusters can heat and cool independently of any single title.

A game can belong to multiple clusters. Its score gets a meta tailwind if its primary cluster is heating, a headwind if it's cooling. The detail page surfaces this on every game.
