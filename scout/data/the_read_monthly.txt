Step back from the noise and the month's pattern is friendslop's specialisation. The cluster's first wave (Lethal Company, Among Us-era survivors) was mechanically simple and price-sensitive. The current wave — R.E.P.O., Schedule I, Backbone Co. — is mechanically richer at the same $10-25 SKU. Retention has roughly doubled on cohort-comparable titles. That changes the unit economics: a solo or trio shop now needs ~30K sustained CCU rather than 100K to fund the next product. The business consequence is consolidation pressure on mid-tier publishers, whose marketing budgets no longer command the alpha they did in 2023. Three studios this month independently turned down publisher capital. That is the structural read.
