The month's structural read is that wishlist counts above 3M are no longer rare. Forza Horizon 6, Subnautica 2, GTA VI, Hogwarts Legacy at its pre-launch peak — all four crossed the threshold. The 3M-wishlist bar used to be once-a-year; it's now four times per year and counting. That changes pre-launch marketing economics: wishlist accumulation as a one-time playable-demo-fed campaign is being replaced by multi-year drip-cohort accumulation. Studios who can't run that long campaign are at a structural disadvantage. The corollary: the highest-EV pre-launch indicator is no longer wishlist count, it's wishlist persistence — does the count hold through delay announcements, sequel reveals, content gaps. That's the new diagnostic.
