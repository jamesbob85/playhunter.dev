Methodology

How the Breakout Score is computed, where every number comes from, and what is still being approximated.

What Scout is

Scout is a daily-refreshed scouting desk for PC games. It ranks breakout candidates — games that may find a step-change in commercial outcome — with explicit confidence and projected scale. It is the forward-looking sister to the playhunter.dev top-1000 dashboard.

It is built for buy-side investors, publishers, and senior developers who need to read a thesis on a title in under sixty seconds and act on it.

The Breakout Score

A 0–100 composite, computed per game. The score blends five signal families weighted by lifecycle stage. Early-stage games lean on intent signals; launched games lean on attention and performance.

score = stage_modifier × Σ ( signal_family × stage_weight )

stages          attention  intent  performance  community  press
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Announced            18%     55%        10%        10%       7%
EA                   30%     22%        28%        13%       7%
Launched             30%     10%        40%        12%       8%

The score delta is the change in score this period. It blends three real-data sources where available: live Twitch burn rate (viewers ÷ followers, log-scaled), revenue 7-day percentage from history, and IGDB hypes for pre-launch products. When all three are flat, a deterministic synthesised delta is used so that Movers always has variance — that fallback retires automatically as snapshot history accumulates.

A maturity dampener applies to launched products with more than $150M lifetime revenue and over a year since release. Their score deltas are multiplied by 0.3 because further breakout is structurally unlikely. They are labelled Settled and excluded from the Movers list by default.

Confidence

Counts how many signal families are firing above a 0.55 threshold. High = three or more. Medium = two. Low = one or zero. The detail page surfaces the 5-family firing dots so you can see exactly which axes are confirming.

Scale band

Bucketed projection of peak concurrent users, derived from current score and stage cohort. Phenom (100–400K), Hit (35–80K), Cult (8–25K), Watch (1–6K), Settled (mature long-tail).

This is a band, not a point estimate. A defensible regression onto past breakouts' arc data is on the roadmap as the comparables library matures.

Snapshot history

Every daily build persists a snapshot of per-game metrics to scout/data/snapshots/YYYY-MM-DD.json. After seven days of snapshots, the scoring engine starts using real week-over-week deltas for followers, wishlists, Twitch attention, IGDB hypes, and revenue. After thirty days, monthly deltas. Snapshots older than sixty days are auto-trimmed.

Scout's voice

Theses are written by Scout — a 20-year games-industry analyst persona, business-first, numerate, dry, slightly contrarian. The system prompt is the source of truth for voice, with a banned-vocab lint pass that rejects hype words on every regeneration.

Hard rules every thesis must follow:

  • Lead with the business read, not fan-speak.
  • Cite one precise comparable with the magnitude gap stated by number.
  • Include a falsifiable "what would change my mind" condition.
  • Reference one cohort median by number.
  • Vary bullet cadence — never three same-shape sentences in a row.

Quality discipline

Every shipped artefact passes a three-lens critique against fixed criteria before publish:

  • Content — info density, defensibility of claims, calibration of numbers.
  • UX — skim cost, hierarchy, action paths.
  • Tone — persona fidelity, lint pass, memorability.

No artefact ships with a lens below 85/100. Three iterations cap; if still failing, the artefact is structurally wrong and gets re-spec'd from scratch.

Honest limitations

  • The universe is currently 20 hand-seeded games for the demo. Production target is 300–800 active candidates.
  • Week-over-week deltas synthesise where snapshot history isn't yet seven days old. The Real-numbers card on each detail page shows the absolute base, which is real.
  • Comparables are referenced narratively but the projected-peak-CCU regression onto labelled past breakouts is on the roadmap.
  • Reddit, Discord, YouTube, TikTok, press, Roblox, and itch.io signals are not yet wired. Their absence is flagged on every signal table.
