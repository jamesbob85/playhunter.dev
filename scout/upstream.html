Upstream

Where taste shifts before the PC cohort catches up. Roblox skews young (taste vector for 2–5 years out). itch.io is the indie incubator most Steam breakouts come from. Treat with a grain of salt — these are taste signals, not direct breakout candidates.

Roblox — top trending experiences

What's currently dominating concurrent attention. Genre and mechanic patterns here often emerge in PC games years later.

itch.io — featured + top-rated

Indies proving a loop before publisher attention or Steam. Itch breakouts have historically crossed to Steam within 6–18 months.

How these feed the scoring

Upstream signals are intentionally not blended directly into game-level Breakout Scores — the noise level is too high. Instead they inform the Meta page's taste-cluster definitions: a mechanic that's hot on Roblox or itch.io that maps to one of our tracked clusters lifts that cluster's tailwind weight. Future builds will surface this attribution on each game's detail page.
