The position

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, surprises

Strengths

42B downloads a year. Nothing else comes close.

Where a mobile SKU exists, Play has it: 130 of 137.

In emerging markets, Play is often the only store.

Weaknesses

71% of watched hours sit on platforms Play doesn't serve. Valve alone holds 10%.

86% of Play's watched hours come from 5+ year-old games. A legacy portfolio.

9.5× less money per download than iOS — and no middle class.

Opportunities

61% of watched love comes from companies already on Play. The SKUs are missing, not the relationships.

8,251 games kept 100+ creators. The tail needs a store, not a spotlight.

1.65M people streamed console games to two viewers — through a share button phones don't have.

Surprises

Hours say Play has 16% of the love. Creators say 26%. The measure changes the story.

Half of all watch time goes to games 8+ years old. The #1 game is 17.

China: 20% of world spend, 84% domestic, zero Play.

Love is measurable. Play's share of it is a choice.
